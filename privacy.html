privacy policy

last updated: 7 october 2026

in short: dotlas collects nothing about you. there is no account, no dotlas server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. your atlas stays on your iphone, and in your own icloud if you turn icloud sync on.

what stays on your iphone

your name, avatar, trips, their places, notes, the places you've lived and your magnets with their pictures are saved inside the app, on your iphone. they are never sent to us, because there is no "us" to send them to: dotlas has no server. they are included in your iphone's own backups, as for any app.

icloud sync

icloud sync is a part of dotlas pro and is off until you turn it on. while it is on, your atlas and your magnets are also kept in your own icloud account, so your other devices can show them. apple stores them there under apple's privacy policy. we cannot see them. the photos you picked for a trip are not part of it. turning sync off, or erasing your atlas, leaves the copy in your icloud; you can remove it in settings → your name → icloud → manage storage.

your photos and the camera

if you allow it, dotlas reads the photos you took during a trip, near its places, to show them on the trip's card. this happens only on your iphone. photos are never uploaded, copied or shared. you can allow or take back access any time in settings → privacy → photos.

the camera is used for two things: to photograph a magnet, and to scan a friend's compare code. a magnet's picture is cut out of its background on your iphone and kept with your atlas. dotlas saves a card, poster or video to your photos only when you tap save.

comparing with a friend

your compare code holds your name, how many trips, places and kilometres you have, and the countries you've been to and have booked. it holds no places, dates, notes or photos. it goes only to the people you show it or send it to, and their dotlas reads it on their iphone. nothing is sent to a server.

purchases

dotlas pro is bought through apple's app store. apple handles the payment under its own privacy policy. dotlas never gets your name, your card or your address; it only learns from apple whether your apple account has dotlas pro.

what goes over the internet

to work, dotlas uses two outside services. neither gets your name or anything that identifies you.

  • apple maps draws the maps, also those on posters and videos, finds the places you search for and the roads of a road trip. these requests go to apple and follow apple's privacy policy.
  • wikipedia and wikimedia commons provide the travel ideas' photos and, for a trip without photos of your own, pictures of famous sights near its places. to find those, dotlas sends the coordinates of the trip's places to wikipedia. these requests follow the wikimedia privacy policy.

exporting and deleting

profile → export atlas saves your atlas as a file. where it goes after that, like files, icloud drive or a message, is up to you. profile → erase atlas, or deleting the app, removes your atlas and your magnets from the iphone.

children

dotlas collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

changes

if this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
