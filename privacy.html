privacy policy

last updated: 25 september 2026

in short: dotlas collects nothing about you. there is no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. your atlas stays on your iphone.

what stays on your iphone

your name, avatar, trips, their places, notes and the places you've lived are saved in one file inside the app, on your iphone. they are never sent to us, because there is no "us" to send them to: dotlas has no server. the file is included in your iphone's own backups, as for any app.

your photos

if you allow it, dotlas reads the photos you took during a trip, near its places, to show them on the trip's card. this happens only on your iphone. photos are never uploaded, copied or shared. you can allow or take back access any time in settings → privacy → photos.

what goes over the internet

to work, dotlas uses two outside services. neither gets your name or anything that identifies you.

  • apple maps draws the map, finds the places you search for and the roads of a road trip. these requests go to apple and follow apple's privacy policy.
  • wikipedia and wikimedia commons provide the travel ideas' photos and, for a trip without photos of your own, pictures of famous sights near its places. to find those, dotlas sends the coordinates of the trip's places to wikipedia. these requests follow the wikimedia privacy policy.

apple intelligence

on iphones with apple intelligence, "ask dotlas" answers with apple's model that runs on your iphone itself. your questions are not sent anywhere and nothing is saved.

exporting and deleting

profile → export atlas saves your atlas as a file. where it goes after that, like files, icloud drive or a message, is up to you. profile → erase atlas, or deleting the app, removes your atlas from the iphone.

children

dotlas collects no personal data from anyone, children included.

changes

if this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
