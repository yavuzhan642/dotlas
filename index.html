your atlas

where are my trips saved?

on your iphone. there is no account and no dotlas server. your atlas is part of your iphone's backups, like your other apps. with dotlas pro you can also keep it in your own icloud.

how do i keep a copy, or move to a new iphone?

in profile → export atlas you can save your whole atlas as a file, to files, icloud drive or anywhere you like. on a new iphone, open dotlas and tap restore an atlas from a file on the first page.

why does dotlas ask for my photos?

so a trip can show the photos you took during it, near its places. they are read on your iphone and never uploaded. you can change this any time in settings → privacy → photos.

"your atlas couldn't be opened." what now?

nothing has been deleted. tap try again first. if it still doesn't open, tap share the file and send it to the address above, so it can be rescued.

how do i delete everything?

tap profile → erase atlas, or delete the app. either removes your atlas from the iphone. what icloud sync put in your icloud stays there until you remove it in settings → your name → icloud → manage storage.

dotlas pro

what is dotlas pro?

the paid part of dotlas: the fridge without a limit, year cards, route posters and videos, comparing with a friend, widgets, icloud sync and detailed stats. everything else stays free. you find it in profile → dotlas pro.

how do i cancel, or change my plan?

a subscription is managed by apple: profile → dotlas pro → manage subscription, or settings → your name → subscriptions. it runs until the end of the time you paid for. a lifetime purchase is paid once and has nothing to cancel.

i bought dotlas pro and it isn't there.

tap profile → dotlas pro → restore purchase. use the apple account you bought it with. for a refund, ask apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

how does icloud sync work?pro

turn it on in profile → icloud sync, on every iphone you use. your trips, places, profile and magnets are kept in your own icloud and are the same on all of them. the photos you picked for a trip stay on the iphone you picked them on.

"icloud is off on this iphone": sign in to icloud in settings and turn on icloud drive, for dotlas too.

the fridge

photograph a magnet you brought home and it hangs on your fridge, cut out of its background. touch and hold a magnet to move it. three magnets are free; dotlas pro has room for every one.

how do i compare with a friend?pro

both open profile → compare with a friend. scan your friend's code with the camera, or send your code as a picture and let them read it from their photos. the code holds your name, your countries and your counts, nothing else.

a widget shows a lock, or old numbers.pro

widgets are a part of dotlas pro. they catch up whenever you open dotlas and leave it again.
