every place you've been, on one map.

dotlas is a travel journal for iphone. add the trips you've been on, the ones you've booked and the places you'd love to go. your trips become dots on the map, with stats, badges and ideas for where to go next.

contact

questions, ideas or a problem? write to dotlasapp@gmail.com. i usually answer within a few days.

questions

where are my trips saved?

only on your iphone. there is no account and no server. your atlas is part of your iphone's backups, like your other apps.

how do i keep a copy, or move to a new iphone?

in profile → export atlas you can save your whole atlas as a file, to files, icloud drive or anywhere you like. on a new iphone, open dotlas and tap restore an atlas from a file on the first page.

why does dotlas ask for my photos?

so a trip can show the photos you took during it, near its places. they are read on your iphone and never uploaded. you can change this any time in settings → privacy → photos.

"your atlas couldn't be opened." what now?

nothing has been deleted. tap try again first. if it still doesn't open, tap share the file and send it to the address above, so it can be rescued.

how do i delete everything?

tap profile → erase atlas, or delete the app. either removes your atlas from the iphone.
